# Drawing on Slides

Draw, highlight and point on your slides while you present. Each time you present is kept as its own **annotation session**, separate from the slides, so the deck itself stays clean and you can come back to any session later.

## Drawing while you present

1. Click **Present** in the top-right toolbar.
2. Press `D`, or click the pen button in the bottom-left corner, to open the pen toolbar.
3. Draw on the slide. Your ink stays on that slide and moves and scales with it.
4. Press `Esc` or click **Done** to go back to changing slides.

| Tool | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| Pen | Draws in the chosen color and width |
| Highlighter | Draws a wide, see-through stroke |
| Eraser (`E`) | Removes each stroke it touches |
| Laser pointer | Draws a red trail that fades away and isn't kept |

The toolbar also has seven colors, three widths, **Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`) and **Clear slide**.

With a stylus, the pen draws and your fingers still swipe between slides. Without one, your finger draws while a tool is on.

### Whiteboard pages

Click **+ Board** to add a blank page right after the current slide, for working something out in front of your audience. While you're on a board, **✕ Board** deletes it. Boards belong to the session, not to the deck.

## Where your ink is saved

Ink is saved with the presentation a moment after each stroke, and the toolbar shows **Saved**. Keep the editor tab open while you present, since the Present window saves through it.

If the editor tab was closed, the toolbar says **Kept on this device** instead. The ink is saved the next time you open the presentation in the editor, in the same browser on the same device.

A session is only kept once it has some ink on it.

## Continuing a session

Clicking **Present** always starts a new session. To keep drawing on an earlier one:

1. Click the **▾** arrow next to **Present**.
2. Under **Continue annotating**, pick one of your five most recent sessions, or click **All sessions…** for the rest.

## Managing sessions

Open **Present ▾ → All sessions…**, or **Export → Export Annotated…**, to see every session, newest first, with how many slides and boards have ink.

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **View** | Presents the slides with that session's ink, without the pen |
| **Continue** | Presents with the pen, adding to that session |
| **PDF** | Prints or saves the slides with the ink as a PDF |
| **HTML** | Downloads the slides with the ink as one HTML file that works offline |
| Trash icon | Deletes the session and its ink, after asking |

Click a session's name to rename it, for example to "Lecture 4, section B".

::: tip
Sessions suit teaching: present to one section, start a new session for the next, and afterwards post each section's annotated slides as a PDF.
:::

## What your ink doesn't change

- Undo and redo in the editor leave ink alone, and restoring an older version keeps your current sessions.
- Version history snapshots, templates made with **Save as template**, and pushes to GitHub leave the ink out.
- Share links, **Presenter Mode** and the regular exports show the slides without ink. Use **Export Annotated…** for a copy with it.
- Templates don't have the pen or sessions.
